Overview

EchoChain is a Web3-based local guide service platform, offering personalized experiences such as travel companions, food tours, and medical assistance, all powered by blockchain technology. EchoChain’s decentralized approach ensures user privacy, data security, and transparency while creating a peer-to-peer ecosystem for local guides and travelers to connect directly.

How EchoChain Works

Through our unique services, we empower both travelers and local guides to build real-time experiences that benefit both parties. Whether it's discovering the city's best spots, tasting exclusive food, or receiving medical aid, EchoChain ensures privacy, security, and seamless transactions.

Tokenomics of $ECH

Overview

$ECH is the native utility token of the EchoChain platform. It facilitates all transactions, rewards users for their participation, and acts as the backbone of our decentralized economy. It empowers users to interact with the platform's services, earn rewards, and participate in governance decisions.

Token Distribution

  • 40%: Community Rewards (Staking, Liquidity Mining, and Incentives)
  • 30%: Ecosystem Fund (Partnerships, Development, and Strategic Initiatives)
  • 15%: Team and Advisors (Vested over 4 years)
  • 10%: Reserve Fund (For future growth and unforeseen needs)
  • 5%: Initial Token Sale (For initial ecosystem growth)

Community & KOLs

Building a Thriving Community

At EchoChain, we understand the importance of community in the success of decentralized platforms. Our community-driven model allows users, local guides, and key opinion leaders (KOLs) to interact, collaborate, and grow the ecosystem together. We empower our community by allowing them to participate in governance decisions, promote platform growth, and earn $ECH tokens for their contributions.

Partnering with KOLs

We work with influential KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders) to expand our reach, raise awareness, and ensure that EchoChain remains at the forefront of the decentralized tourism and travel industry. Through collaborations, KOLs can help educate, onboard, and incentivize new users to the platform.
